## The setup

- **Access through groups, not people**: read-only Entra ID groups replaced per-user grants on the data lake.
- **One Fabric F2 capacity** with 6 workspaces, one central and five per business area, and 3 shortcuts to the existing ADLS Gen2.
- **A shared Spark pool** with high concurrency, session timeouts and capacity monitoring.
- **Runbook, hands-on sessions and handover** so the client's IT could administer it alone.

## The trade-off

Instead of buying a bigger capacity, tuning the shared pool kept several developers working on F2, the smallest tier. The client only moved to F4 when new analysts joined.

## Results

Formal acceptance from the client, and two new leads for the consultancy.
