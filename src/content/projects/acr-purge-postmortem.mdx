## What happened

As part of a FinOps effort, we enabled a retention purge on the client's Azure Container Registry. Some of the images it deleted were still running in production, and pods that were rescheduled couldn't pull them anymore. Around 70 active customers of the platform were potentially affected, through 2 services out of roughly 500 repositories.

## Investigation

A five-whys analysis led to the root cause: **production and development shared the same tag lifecycle**, so the purge couldn't tell a stale dev image from one production still needed.

The first workaround looked like it worked, and that was the trap: the pod had landed on a node that still had the image in its local cache. When it moved, it failed again. Validating tags one by one wasn't an option at that scale (tens of thousands of tags).

## Recovery and fixes

The images were rebuilt and the purge suspended; about 14 hours passed from first symptoms to recovery. Afterwards:

- Separate tags for production images.
- A review of retention rules.
- Alerts on `ImagePullBackOff` and `ErrImagePull`.
- Change management for operations like this one.

The purge is back on, and safe.
