## What happened

An account with delegated access to customer environments was compromised. The attacker used that access to run commands on virtual machines in several customer tenants. Containment took four days, and the assessment of every environment was complete ten days after the compromise.

## My part

I was on the front line of the response, not leading the technical war room:

- Analysed logs and identified exposed resources and credentials.
- Prioritized key rotation and blocking public access, some of it executed by the customers' own teams.
- Talked to customers directly.
- Used agentic development to consolidate logs from several sources into **200 reports, one per affected customer**, each with an executive and a technical version.

## Results

All affected customers were prioritized, contacted and supported in about two weeks. In one case, the report was in the customer's hands 4 hours 39 minutes after the meeting where I committed to it.
