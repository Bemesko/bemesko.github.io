At Vetta, part of SMS Group, I worked on Viridis, MES (manufacturing execution system) software used by major steelmakers. The observability work covered its Kubernetes clusters on AKS and on-prem.

## What I built

- **Auto-instrumentation with OpenTelemetry**, so services emit traces and metrics without code changes.
- **Self-service backends**: Grafana for metrics, Graylog for logs and Jaeger for traces, available to teams across AKS and on-prem clusters.
- **GitOps with ArgoCD**, replacing direct Helm deploys. The approach was taken up by SMS Group's cloud team in Germany.

In parallel, Azure and Azure DevOps optimizations cut cloud costs by **15% in my first three months** and reduced pipeline support tickets.

I later turned this work into a talk, _Observability on Kubernetes with OpenTelemetry_, at a Codecon Meetup in 2025.
